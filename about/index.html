About True Finds

True Finds helps you buy with confidence. Sinclair, your free AI shopping companion, checks pricing, points you to stores you can trust, and lets you know when it matters.

Why we built it

Shopping got noisy. Red price tags, countdown timers and “was” prices are everywhere, and they rarely tell you what you really need to know: is this a good price for this product?

So we built a companion that checks. Sinclair looks at a product’s own price history and gives you one plain answer: a real deal, a typical price, a discount that isn’t what it claims, or not enough history yet to say. No chart to puzzle over.

Who we’re for

US shoppers who know what they want, value their time, and would rather buy once and buy right, plus the families they shop for.

What you’ll find here

  • Price checks. Paste a product link or name and Sinclair tells you whether today’s price is really low.
  • Real Deals. Discounts Sinclair has checked against real price history.
  • Stores we’ve checked. Independent stores with honest product pages and a clear button to buy.
  • Guides. Plain, sourced advice on real deals, shipping cutoffs, returns, gifts and more, new every day.
  • Gifts and lists. Ideas for the people (and pets) you love, and lists for life’s big moments.
  • A free Chrome extension that checks the page you’re on with one click.
  • Help that keeps up with you. Sinclair lets you know what matters to you, and keeps learning as your life changes. The rest comes in your weekly summary.

How we make money

We keep this simple and in the open.

  • Commissions from stores. When you buy through our links, True Finds may earn a commission from the store, at no cost to you. Sinclair’s verdicts are always free and independent.
  • Members (Oct 11). Some shoppers choose our paid plans for extra help, like gift plans and reminders.
  • Sponsors. Sponsored placements are always labeled. Sponsors never change a verdict. Verdicts are not for sale.
And one promise: we will never sell your personal information.
