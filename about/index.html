How we make money

We keep this simple and in the open. Free to ask. Free to save. Free to buy.

  • Commissions from stores. When you buy through our links, TrueFinds may earn a commission from the store, at no cost to you.
  • Creators share in it. When you shop a creator’s list, part of what we earn goes to that creator.
  • Members. Some shoppers choose our paid plans for extra help, like gift plans and reminders.
  • Sponsors. Sponsored placements are always labeled. Sponsors do not influence recommendations.

The details are on how TrueFinds earns money.

And one promise: we will never sell your personal information.
