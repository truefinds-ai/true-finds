TrueFindsBot, Sinclair’s agent

Sinclair, your shopping agent, helps shoppers find the right product at a fair price from a store they can trust. This page says what our agent reads from online stores, when, and how to reach us.

How to recognize it

  • User agent: TrueFindsBot/1.0 (+https://truefinds.ai/bot)
  • Requests are signed with Web Bot Auth (HTTP Message Signatures). The Signature-Agent header is "https://truefinds.ai", and our public key is at https://truefinds.ai/.well-known/http-message-signatures-directory.
  • Requests come from our servers on Vercel, in the United States.

What it reads, and when

  • AI Readiness by Sinclair. When a store asks for an AI Readiness check, and in a weekly recheck for those stores, the agent reads that store’s public pages (home page, product pages, the public product list, policy pages, robots.txt, sitemap and agents.md) and asks the store’s own agent endpoints a few shopping questions, the way an AI shopping assistant would.
  • The agent test cart. As part of that check, the agent puts one product in a checkout to see shipping options, the total and the payment methods offered, then cancels it. It never completes a purchase and never sends payment details, a name, an email or a street address.
  • Price history. For stores in our price record, a recorder reads the public product list about once a day, one page at a time with short pauses, to keep the price history behind Sinclair’s verdicts.
  • Store facts. For the same stores, once a week, we read the return and shipping policy pages, so Sinclair’s answers quote the store’s own words.

An AI Readiness check makes a few dozen requests to one store over about a minute. The other jobs make far fewer.

robots.txt and crawl delay

Before reading a page, the agent checks the store’s robots.txt and skips any path disallowed for TrueFindsBot or for all agents. If the store sets a Crawl-delay, the agent waits that long between requests. If robots.txt cannot be read because of a server error, the agent reads nothing from that store until it can.

What it never does

  • It never buys anything, logs in, fills in forms or posts reviews.
  • It never collects shopper data from a store.
  • It never reads pages behind a login.
And one promise: we will never sell your personal information.

Questions, or to ask us to stop

Write to hello@truefinds.ai with your store’s web address, and we will answer within two business days. If you want our agent to stop reading your store, say so and we will stop.
