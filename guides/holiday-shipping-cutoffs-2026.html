Holiday 2026 shipping: when to order so gifts arrive on time

By the True Finds team · October 6, 2026

A great gift that arrives on December 28 is still a great gift. It just isn't the moment you planned.

The good news: a few dates and one simple sum tell you exactly when to order. Here they are.

The Postal Service's 2026 dates

If you are mailing a package yourself within the lower 48 states, the Postal Service recommends sending by these dates for delivery before December 25:

  • USPS Ground Advantage: Thursday, December 17
  • First-Class Mail (cards and letters): Thursday, December 17
  • Priority Mail: Friday, December 18
  • Priority Mail Express: Saturday, December 19

Sending to Alaska or Hawaii? Ground Advantage moves up a day, to Wednesday, December 16. The other dates stay the same.

FedEx's 2026 dates

FedEx lists the last days to ship for delivery by Thursday, December 24:

  • FedEx Ground and Home Delivery: depends on how far the package travels. Thursday, December 17 for a 5-day trip, Friday, December 18 for 4 days, Monday, December 21 for 3 days, Tuesday, December 22 for 2 days, and Wednesday, December 23 for 1 day.
  • FedEx Ground Economy: Tuesday, December 15
  • FedEx Express Saver: Monday, December 21
  • FedEx 2Day: Tuesday, December 22
  • FedEx Overnight services: Wednesday, December 23

Not sure how many days your package needs? FedEx's ground transit map on fedex.com shows it by ZIP code.

Christmas falls on a Friday this year, so those last few days before it get busy. Mailing a week earlier than the deadline takes the worry out of it.

Ordering online? The store's clock matters more

When you order a gift from an online store, the carrier's deadline is only half the story. Two other things come first:

  • Processing time. Many stores take one to three business days to pack an order before it ships.
  • The store's own delivery window. Most stores list it on their shipping page, such as "5 to 7 business days."

Some smaller stores quote a range like 7 to 14 business days, and say it can stretch to two or three weeks at peak season. That is worth knowing in November, not in December.

Work out your own order-by date in one minute

Use the slow end of the store's window, never the fast end.

  1. Pick the day you want the gift in hand. We suggest Wednesday, December 23, two days before Christmas.
  2. Add the store's processing time to the slow end of its delivery window.
  3. Count back that many business days, skipping weekends.

Example: a store packs in up to 2 business days and delivers in 5 to 7 business days. That is 9 business days. Counting back from Wednesday, December 23, your order-by date is Thursday, December 10.

At peak season: if a store says delivery can take up to three weeks, count back 15 business days. That puts your order-by date at Wednesday, December 2.

Five habits that save the holiday

  • Read the shipping page before you buy, not after. It is usually linked at the bottom of the store's site.
  • Check whether the store offers gift wrap or a gift message, and whether it can ship straight to the person you are buying for. It saves you a trip to the post office.
  • Ask for a gift receipt so the recipient can exchange it without showing the price.
  • Look up the holiday return window. Many stores extend returns for gifts bought in November and December. Others do not, so it is worth a look.
  • Order the hard-to-find gifts first. Popular items sell out in December even when shipping would still be on time.

Quick checklist

  • Mailing it yourself: send by December 17 for USPS Ground Advantage, December 18 for Priority Mail; FedEx Ground by December 17 for the longest trips.

  • Read the shipping and return pages before you buy.
