Is that Prime Big Deal Days discount real? A five-minute check

Prime Big Deal Days runs Tuesday, October 6 and Wednesday, October 7, with Target and Walmart running their own sales the same week. Some red price tags are real savings. Many are not new lows at all. Here is how to tell the difference in about five minutes.

By the True Finds team · Updated October 5, 2026
IN 30 SECONDS
  • ✓Most sale badges are not new lows. In one study, about 1 in 8 price changes was a genuine new low.
  • ✓Judge a deal against what the product usually sells for, not the "was" price.
  • ✓Five minutes is enough: price, usual price, compare, one other store, then decide.

Close to nine in ten price changes were not the best price that product had seen. That does not make them bad prices. It means the sale badge alone does not tell you much.

2

What makes a discount honest

The Federal Trade Commission has a plain test for "was" prices:

A discount is dressed up when the "was" price was raised for a short time so the sale could look bigger. So the question is never "how big is the percentage?" It is "what did this usually sell for?"

3

The five-minute check

  1. 1
    Ignore the percentage. Look at the price."55% off" means nothing until you know what it is 55 percent off. Write down the price you would actually pay, including shipping.
  2. 2
    Find the usual price.Amazon now shows a Price History view on many product pages, with up to 365 days of prices. Look at where the price sat most of the time, not at the highest point on the chart.
  3. 3
    Compare against the usual price, not the "was" price.Clearly below where it usually sits is a real saving. A price you can see it hit last month is a fair price, just not a special one.
  4. 4
    Check one other store.Target Circle Deal Days runs October 6 and 7, and Walmart Deal Days runs October 5 to 11. Sinclair can compare stores for you at truefinds.ai.
  5. 5
    Decide if you need it now.A gift, or something you already planned to buy, at a fair price is a fine reason to buy. An impulse with a fake discount is a good reason to wait. Black Friday is only weeks away.
4

A real deal versus a dressed-up one

GENUINE DEALA coffee maker that usually sells for one price most of the year drops well below it for two days. The "was" price matches what you saw it selling for in August.
INFLATEDA coffee maker shows "was" at a price it only held for a week in September, then drops back to its normal price with a big red badge. Nothing changed except the label.

Both carry the same badge. Only one saves you money.

5

Three shortcuts that help

Tip
Lightning deals and limited-time drops are built to make you hurry. Take the five minutes anyway. A real deal is still a real deal after you check it.
Tip
Bundles and multipacks can hide the unit price. Divide the price by the number of items and compare that.
Tip
Store brands and look-alike listings often sit next to name brands with similar photos. Check the brand name and the seller before you add to cart.
