Ask Muse whether a deal is real before it buys anything for you. Sinclair, your AI shopping companion from True Finds, answers from the product's own price history, in one plain sentence.

How to connect

  1. Open Muse in the Meta AI app or on the web.
  2. Paste the prompt above and send it.
  3. Muse builds the connection and tests it. When it asks, tell it to save.

No account, no sign in and no payment details. The connection only answers questions; it never buys anything.

What to ask

Once TrueFinds is connected, ask in your own words. A few that work well:

"Is this a real deal?" and paste a product link

You get one of four verdicts: GENUINE DEAL, INFLATED, TYPICAL PRICE or NOT ENOUGH DATA, with a one sentence reason, how many days of price history it rests on, and a link to the full record.

"Should I buy this now or wait?"

Sinclair tells you where today's price sits against what the product has actually sold for. When the record is too thin to judge, it says so rather than guessing.

"Where else can I buy Eydology?"

For brands we record, TrueFinds points you to a store that sells the brand directly or carries it. Some of these links may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you, and the answer says so.

What the connection does, and what it never does

It reads the product link or brand you ask about and answers. It asks for no name, email, account or payment details, and it cannot place orders.

Sinclair’s verdicts are always free and independent. A product that earns us nothing gets the same reading as one that does. Read more in our privacy policy, or write to hello@truefinds.ai.
