What True Finds does

Sinclair, your AI shopping companion, reads a product's recorded price history and gives one plain verdict: GENUINE DEAL, INFLATED, TYPICAL PRICE or NOT ENOUGH DATA. The AI connection answers the same questions for an assistant you have chosen to connect.
