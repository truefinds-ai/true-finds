Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Making a list is free, and so is saving it. You only pay the store for what you buy.

Do I need an account?

No. Paste a link and your list is ready. Add your email only if you want the list saved.

Which stores?

Stores we trust, including Amazon, with fair prices and clear shipping and return policies.

Do creators get credit?

Yes. Every list credits the video it came from. Creators who join TrueFinds get their own page, their picks on their own links, and earn when you buy.
